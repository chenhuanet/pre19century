六个主题Six themes

同一批 300 件作品，换六种问法：信仰如何成像，山水如何成为自我，人物如何留下名字，城市如何记录日常，文字如何参与观看，材料如何决定画法。The same 300 works, asked six ways: how faith becomes an image, how landscape becomes a self, how a person keeps a name, how a city records the everyday, how writing joins looking, and how material decides method.
