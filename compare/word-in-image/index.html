手卷与手抄本Scroll and Manuscript

手卷和手抄本都把阅读当成身体动作：展开、停顿、回看。文字与图像不是两件事，而是同一套节奏。Handscroll and manuscript both make reading a bodily act: unfold, pause, return. Word and image are not two things but one rhythm.
