怎样读这 442 件How to read 442 works

这不是一部完整的全球绘画史。它是一份从 400 年到 1799 年的可检索样本：442 件作品，按 10 个地域、七个时间段和六个主题交叉编排。每件作品都能回到详情页，核对馆藏、来源、授权和图像版本。This is not a complete global history of painting. It is a searchable sample from 400 to 1799: 442 works, cross-arranged by 10 regions, seven periods and six themes. Every work leads back to a detail page with its holding, source, licence and image record.

为什么是这 442 件Why these 442

目标是“可进入”，不是“求全”。选择优先覆盖不同文明与媒介的转折点，再补足同一主题下的对照作品；欧洲 150 件、中国 125 件、南亚 97 件、东亚 39 件，其余地域保留少量关键样本。它不是馆藏目录，也不是拍卖图录，更不代表某一地域的全部绘画成就。The aim is access, not completeness. Selection favours turning points across civilizations and media, then adds contrasting works within the same theme: Europe 150, China 125, South Asia 97, East Asia 39, with smaller key samples from other regions. It is neither a museum catalogue nor an auction record, and it does not represent all painting from any one region.

所有计数以图片站的 442 条主记录为准；同一件作品的不同局部、背面或版本会作为额外图像条目保留，因此图像条目总数为 635，而不是 442。All counts refer to the 442 primary records on the image site. Details, versos and versions of the same work are kept as additional image records, so the total is 635 rather than 442.

怎么读一件作品How to read a work

  • 先看日期与题名：日期可能是创作年、纪年或摹本年代，详情页会写明依据。Start with the date and title: the date may be the creation year, a reign date or the date of a copy; the detail page states the basis.
  • 再看馆藏与来源：holding 是今天收藏它的机构，source 是这张数字图来自哪里，二者不是一回事。Then read holding and source: holding is the institution that keeps the work today; source is where this digital image came from. They are not the same thing.
  • 最后看授权与版本：公版标记不等于所有复制件都无风险；商业使用前以详情页的 licence 与来源页为准。Finally read the licence and version: a public-domain mark does not make every reproduction risk-free; check the detail page licence and source page before commercial use.
